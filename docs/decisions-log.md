| D23 | 2026-10-10 | Projects division rules: a job's revenue is its quoted price; no cancelled jobs; jobs unfinished at the as-of date (2026-09-30) are in_progress with only the costs incurred so far; some quotes and jobs use the duplicate customer codes | Model discounts, variations and cancellations | Keeps margins easy to trace. | D24 | 2026-10-10 | Products division rules: about 40 percent of orders come from a quote, the rest are walk-in sales; no cancelled orders; stock leaves only when an order is delivered; stock is bought once a quarter and never goes below zero; `job_use` stock movements are not generated yet | Model purchase orders, cancellations and stock used on jobs | Keeps the stock ledger traceable. | D25 | 2026-10-10 | Rentals are built asset by asset: each asset gets its own run of rentals sized to its utilisation target, and rentals of different assets that start on the same day and last the same time are grouped into one booking. | D26 | 2026-10-10 | Rental quotes: about a quarter of bookings carry a won quote, decided 1 to 14 days before the rental starts; lost, expired and open quotes are added so the win rate matches the register; quote cost is a share of price (crew, handling, transport) and exists only on the quote.
